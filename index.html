  Postdoctoral Researcher · Chamorro Group · University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

Hi, I am a postdoctoral researcher in Prof. Chamorro's group at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My research focuses on experimental fluid mechanics, investigating complex flow phenomena underlying pollutant and sediment transport, as well as energy-related processes in aquatic and atmospheric systems. I am also interested in advancing optical diagnostic techniques and applying data-driven methods to enable more accurate and comprehensive flow measurements and analysis.

Research Interests

Environmental Transport

Pollutant and sediment transport in aquatic and atmospheric flows.

Energy-Related Flows

Flow processes underlying energy systems in natural environments.

Optical Diagnostics

Advancing optical measurement techniques for complex flows.

Data-Driven Methods

Data-driven analysis for more accurate and comprehensive flow measurements.
